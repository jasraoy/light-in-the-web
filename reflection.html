My Personal Reflection

Learning to become a better person through faith, honesty, and responsible actions.

✦

What I Have Learned About Myself

As a student, I know that I still have many things to learn. There are days when I make mistakes or face problems, but these experiences help me understand myself better. My faith reminds me to keep trusting God, especially when things do not happen the way I expect.

I have also learned that being a good person is not only about what I do in front of others. It also matters how I treat people when I am online. Sometimes, a simple comment or post can affect someone's feelings. That is why I want to be more careful with my words and think before sharing anything on the internet.

I cannot say that I always make the right decisions, because I am still growing and learning. What matters to me is that I accept my mistakes and try to do better next time. I want my actions to show respect, honesty, and kindness, even in small ways.

For me, being a light to others does not mean being perfect. It means choosing to do what is right, helping when I can, and keeping my faith in God. I hope that through my everyday actions, I can make a good difference in someone's life.

A PROMISE TO MYSELF

How I Want to Use Technology

I will do my best to become a responsible internet user. I will be careful about what I post, respect other people's opinions, and avoid using technology to spread hate or false information. I also want to use what I learn online for good purposes.

I know I cannot control everything on the internet, but I can control my own actions. I want to use technology in a way that reflects my faith and the values I believe in.
